Artifact A · Diagnose & rewrite

Pairs: mark which quality attributes fail, then rewrite as a shall statement + one acceptance check.

R1.1 — Users should be able to log in to the app using their credentials and the system should keep them safe.

ambiguous (“safe”) two requirements glued not verifiable as written
Example split.
FR: The system shall authenticate a user with email and password before granting access to account data.
NFR: The system shall store passwords only as salted hashes (e.g. Argon2/bcrypt); plaintext passwords shall never be logged.
Acceptance: Given a valid credential pair, access is granted within 2s; given an invalid pair, access is denied and no session is created.

R2.1 — Teachers should be able to give grades to students and students should be able to see how they are doing in the course.

two stakeholders, two capabilities “how they are doing” vague
Example.
R-T: A teacher shall assign a numeric grade (0–100) to an enrolled student for a graded item.
R-S: An enrolled student shall view their own grades for each graded item in a course, updated within 5 minutes of a teacher save.
Acceptance: Student A never sees Student B’s grades; teacher change is visible to the student after refresh within the SLA.

From last week’s introspection: “Send a reminder 24 hours before the exam.”

solution, not problem channel unspecified whose clock?
Problem-space first.
The system shall notify each registered examinee of the definitive exam time and room at least 24 hours before the scheduled start, using the contact channel on file.
Acceptance: If room or time changes within that window, a second notification is sent within 15 minutes of the change being saved.
Note: “reminder” is one mechanism; the requirement states the information obligation.
